Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Royal Harvest ("we", "us") delivers fresh fruits, vegetables and daily essentials in Gadag, Betageri and Hulkoti, Karnataka. This page explains what information we collect when you use our website and app at royalharvest.co.in, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your name and mobile number, to create your account and contact you about your order.
  • Delivery addresses you save, and a map pin if you share one, so we can deliver to the right place.
  • Your orders: items, amounts, delivery time and status.
  • A device identifier stored in your browser, which helps us keep your account signed in and prevent misuse such as repeated fake sign-in attempts.
  • Notification settings, only if you choose to turn on order alerts on your device.

Sign-in codes on WhatsApp

To confirm your mobile number, we may send a 6-digit code to your number on WhatsApp. The message is sent through the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. We store only a scrambled (hashed) form of the code, and it expires after 10 minutes.

How we use it

  • To accept, pack, deliver and support your orders.
  • To contact you about an order, such as a delay, a substitution or a delivery update.
  • To keep the service safe and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To understand which products are popular so we can stock better.

We do not sell your personal information. We will not send you marketing messages without asking you first.

Who can see it

  • Our team and delivery partners see your name, phone number, address and order, only as needed to deliver it.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: our cloud database and hosting providers (Supabase and Cloudflare), and Meta for WhatsApp sign-in codes. They handle the data only to provide those services.
  • Authorities, if the law requires us to share information.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and order history while your account is active and as long as needed for accounts, refunds and legal records. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your choices

  • Ask us to correct or delete your information, or tell us you no longer want to be contacted.
  • Turn off notifications in your device or browser settings at any time.
  • Clear your browser data to remove the device identifier stored on your phone.

To make a request, call or message us on the number below. We will reply within a reasonable time, normally within 7 days.

Children

Our service is meant for adults placing grocery orders. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
