Delivery, Cancellation & Refunds

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Delivery

  • Where: Gadag, Betageri and Hulkoti, Karnataka.
  • When: 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM, all days including Sundays.
  • How fast: usually 20–30 minutes if you choose "Now". You can also pick a slot today or tomorrow.
  • Minimum order: ₹50. Delivery is free on orders of ₹250 and above; below that, a delivery fee of ₹15 applies. The fee is shown before you place the order.
  • After you order, you can follow your rider on a live map. If your order is delayed, we will contact you on your mobile number.
  • Please keep your phone with you. If we cannot reach you or find the address, we may have to cancel the order, and we may not be able to re-deliver fresh items.

Cancelling an order

  • You can cancel before your order is packed or has left for delivery. Call or message us as soon as possible on the number below.
  • Once the order is out for delivery, it usually cannot be cancelled, because fresh items cannot be restocked.
  • We may cancel an order if an item is unavailable or we cannot deliver to your address. We will tell you, and you will not be charged.

Fresh, damaged or wrong items

If an item is not fresh, is damaged or is not what you ordered, tell us within 2 hours of delivery and send a photo. We will either replace the item, give you a credit, or refund the item's price. We decide with you which works best.

  • Please keep the item and packaging until we reply.
  • Natural differences in size, shape or colour of fresh produce are not defects.
  • For safety and hygiene, we do not take back fresh produce, dairy or other perishable items unless there is a quality problem.

Refunds

  • Paid in cash or UPI to the rider: we return the amount for the affected items by UPI, or adjust it on your next order, as you prefer.
  • Online payments (when we offer them): approved refunds go back to the original payment method, normally within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.
