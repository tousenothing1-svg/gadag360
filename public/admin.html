💰 Profit
Profit = everything customers paid (products, delivery charges and tips) − your cost of the products − the tips, which are for your delivery boys. "Your cost" comes from Products as it was when each order came in; money for products that had no cost then isn't counted. Cancelled orders don't count.

Products
#PhotoNameCategoryCustomer price (₹)Your cost (₹)Market price (₹)Unit · sizesStock leftStatusAction
Loading...
Tap a photo to take or choose a new one. The price is for the unit (for example ₹40 for 1kg); other sizes are worked out from it. "Your cost" is what the vendor charges you for the same unit: only you see it here, customers never do. Profit is the customer price minus your cost, and the % is how much of the customer price you keep. "Stock left" counts units: 20 × 500g is 10kg. Selling 1kg of it takes 2 off, 250g takes 0.5, and at 0 the product shows as out of stock by itself. Deleted products wait in 🗑️ Deleted Products, where you can bring them back.
🗑️ Deleted Products
Products you delete wait here. Customers can't see or order them. Tap ↩️ Back to application to put one back in Products and in the shop, with the same price, sizes, stock and photo.
